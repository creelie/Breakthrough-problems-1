# Priority check: Wormald's Conjectures 2 and 6 on Hamilton cycles in tournaments

Question: had anyone proved or refuted Conjecture 2 (the limit of ρ_R(R_d) over odd d exists) or
Conjecture 6 (for every regular tournament, H ≠ 0 and d/H is the square of a rational) of
N. C. Wormald, *Tournaments with many Hamilton cycles*, or identified his constant 2.855958?

The paper has been on Wormald's Monash web page since at least 2013
(https://users.monash.edu.au/~nwormald/papers/hamtourn.pdf, server date 11 February 2013) and was
posted to arXiv as [arXiv:2610.16245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16245) on 12 October 2026.

Checked 14 October 2026, 09:10–09:16 UTC (an earlier pass on the same day gave the same result).

| Source | Search | Result |
|---|---|---|
| OpenRefute (github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing) | issues matching `Wormald`; issues matching `tournament` | none for `Wormald`; the five `tournament` hits are about link-irregular tournaments, Girão et al. on flashes and rainbows, and project bookkeeping |
| arXiv API | `all:"Hamilton cycles" AND all:tournaments`, `all:Wormald AND all:tournament`, newest first | arXiv:2610.16245 itself; the other hits (Hamilton decompositions, oriented discrepancy, transversal cycles, Walecki tournaments, Jackson's conjecture) do not treat Wormald's Hessian or his constant |
| Semantic Scholar | papers citing arXiv:2610.16245 | the paper is not yet indexed |
| Zenodo | `"Wormald" AND tournament`; `"Hamilton cycles" AND tournament AND Hessian` | 0 records each |
| Web search | the conjectures, "d/H perfect square", the constant 2.855958 with cosh 1 and cos 1 | only Wormald's paper (Monash PDF, arXiv abstract and HTML) and unrelated work on Hamilton cycles in tournaments (Cuckler; Kühn–Osthus surveys; Calkin–Novick–Ushijima-Mwesigwa on Moser's question) |

Conclusion: as of 14 October 2026 we found no proof or refutation of either conjecture and no
identification of the constant. Wormald's own paper reports the conjectures as open, with the
square property observed numerically and the constant estimated as 2.8559579 by polynomial fits.


## Update, 25 October 2026

The search above is a historical record as of 14 October, not a present claim that the broader asymptotic maximum remains open. Divoux and Wang's preprint arXiv:2610.23755, posted 16 October 2026, gives the sharp global Hamilton-cycle and path asymptotics with the same constant. The current manuscript cites it and removes the old open-problem sentence. Its exact finite Hessian calculation is derived directly from Wormald's definition, and no priority conclusion about the broader theorem or constant is inferred from this dated search log.
